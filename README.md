## Our approach

**Design for control.** Boundaries, permissions, and human approval come before autonomous execution.

**Build for inspection.** Decisions, tool actions, retrieval, and evaluations should be traceable.

**Validate before claiming.** Reproducible tests, operational checks, and deployment evidence determine readiness.

**Keep the mission visible.** We Do Care Global maintains a founder-level pledge to dedicate **33% of annual corporate revenue** to financial and material assistance for vulnerable people worldwide. This is a commitment, **not a claim of donations already made**. [Read the humanitarian constitution](CONSTITUTION_33PCT.md).
