# We Do Care Global — 33% Humanitarian Constitution (founder-level commitment)

> Established by founder Emir Perla, Sarajevo, Bosnia and Herzegovina.
> Status: constitutional pledge (not yet audited disbursement). This file makes the pledge verifiable.

## Article 1 — Pledge
33% of annual corporate revenue of We Do Care Global is permanently dedicated to direct
financial and material assistance to vulnerable individuals worldwide, regardless of
geographic boundaries or social background. Every person deprived of basic necessities
has the right to support for a dignified life.

## Article 2 — Definitions
- **Revenue**: gross corporate revenue per fiscal year, as reported in annual accounts.
- **Assistance**: direct transfers (cash, goods, medical, shelter, education) to individuals;
  operating costs and marketing do not count.
- **Vulnerable**: individuals deprived of basic necessities for a dignified life.

## Article 3 — Transparency (to become fully legit)
1. Publish annual revenue figure + 33% amount + recipient ledger (anonymized) within 90 days of year-end.
2. Publish wallet / bank reference for the 33% fund.
3. Independent review when revenue exceeds BAM 100,000/year.
4. If revenue = 0 (pre-revenue, current stage), publish `DISBURSEMENTS_YYYY.csv` with `0` and explanation.
