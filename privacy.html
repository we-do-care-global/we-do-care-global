Privacy notice

How this website handles visits and contact inquiries.

Website visits

This version has no analytics beacon, advertising tracker, application cookie or browser-storage tracking in its website code. Fonts and media are served with the website. Hosting providers may process technical request data such as IP addresses and access logs to deliver and protect the site. This notice does not claim control over provider logs.

Contact

Inquiry fields are used in your browser to prepare an email. They are not submitted to a website form server. Copying uses your device clipboard; downloading creates a text file on your device. Sending through your chosen email service shares the information with that service and the recipient. Email correspondence is used to respond to your inquiry. Avoid passwords, patient data and confidential information.

External services

GitHub, Zenodo, ORCID and application previews have their own data practices when you open their links. The AgentGuard page contacts a public Cloudflare Workers reference service only when you click a check; this exposes normal network request information to that service. Public application previews are separate from this informational website.

Questions or requests

For questions about information you have sent or requests concerning it, email emir@wedocare-global.com.
